# Privacy Policy for SurfBreak

**Effective Date:** May 14, 2026

Hi, I'm the developer of SurfBreak. I built this tool to solve my own struggle with mindless scrolling, and I'm sharing it freely to help the community stay focused. Because I value my own privacy, I’ve built SurfBreak to respect yours.

### 1. The Solo Developer Promise: No Data Sales
I do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information to others. In fact, I don't even have a database to store it in. My mission is to help you reclaim your time, not to monetize your browsing habits.

### 2. Zero-Data Architecture (Local Only)
SurfBreak is designed to stay completely on your machine.
* **No Servers:** Your blocklists, tasks, and Focus Scores are stored locally in your browser using the Chrome Storage API.
* **No Accounts:** You don't need to sign up or log in.
* **No Tracking:** I do not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry to monitor what you do.

### 3. How Data is Handled
Since all data is local, you are the sole owner of it. 
* **Control:** You can see, edit, or wipe your data at any time within the extension settings.
* **Erasure:** Uninstalling the extension immediately and permanently deletes all your SurfBreak data from your device.

### 4. Third-Party Disclaimer
SurfBreak is hosted on the Google Chrome Web Store. Google may provide me with "Aggregated Data" (such as how many people installed the app this week), but this never contains personal info that could identify you.
